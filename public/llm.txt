## Writing (full notes)

### Finlio.app — 5 Aug 2026 — /writing/finlio

Finlio is a personal finance teammate. One view of your money. A brief before the market opens.

He kept opening five apps to answer one question: what do I actually have. A bank here, a broker there, something remembered at tax time. The numbers were never missing. They were never in the same room.

Finlio is the thing he wanted on those nights. Not another tracker abandoned in three weeks. A quiet teammate that keeps the picture together and taps when something in his own money is about to matter.

He is building it as a founder and as the person who writes the code. Beny and he started from ordinary frustration.

If you grew up around Indian money, you already know the split: savings in one place, SIPs in another, EPF in a portal visited twice a year. Apps are fine at showing a number. They go quiet when that number is about to change.

He did not want a dashboard that waits for discipline. He wanted something that shows up in the morning and says, in regular English, why the things he already owns might move today. That daily note is the first habit.

Most of his day is engineering: calm interfaces, systems that do not flinch. The founder part is deciding what not to build. Money is personal, so the default is local, readable, and easy to take with you. Cloud is a choice, not the price of entry. Web first. Phones come when the desk product is already good.

Finlio is early. Live at https://www.finlio.app. Two people close to the problem. He is not replacing a broker. He is not telling anyone what to buy. He is trying to stop you from being the integration layer between your own accounts.

### ARIA — 22 Jul 2026 — /writing/aria

ARIA is a research assistant that answers with sources, not vibes. He works on it as an AI Engineer at The Binary Holdings.

He did not start this product. He joined a team that already understood the problem: intelligence work falls apart when you cannot see where an answer came from.

His job is to keep the model honest. Retrieve first. Speak second. Leave a trail a person can follow.

Most search dumps links. Most chat sounds sure when it is guessing. ARIA sits in the middle: find the material that supports an answer, then say what it used.

He works on the AI side: how a question is understood, how context is gathered, how a response stays tied to evidence. Citations a human can open. Answers that get quieter when sources are thin. He will not unpack internals here. If they cannot show the work, they should not ship the sentence.

The value is not a flashy reply. It is a person who can keep asking follow-ups and still land on something they can defend. If ARIA does its job, research gets faster without getting sloppy.
